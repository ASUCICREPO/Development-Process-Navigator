Help Center

Quick guides to help you get the most out of the Development Process Navigator.

  1. Register with your ASU email — no access code needed for students.
  2. After logging in, you'll land on the Student Dashboard.
  3. To join a class, ask your instructor for a Join Code or wait for an email invite.

💡 Quick Tips

  • Look for the ⓘ icons throughout the app — hover over them for contextual help.
  • Exercises can be attempted up to two times — use your first attempt to learn, then resubmit for a better score.
  • Instructors: share your Exercise ID or link with students. They can also find assigned exercises on their dashboard.
  • Need help? Contact your instructor or reach out to the ASU CIC team.
