## Getting Started

### Creating an Account

1. Open the app and choose your role — **Student** or **Instructor**
2. Click **Register**
3. Fill in your email, password (min 8 chars, include a letter and number), and display name

**Students:**
- If your instructor gave you a **Join Code**, enter it to auto-enroll in their class
- You can skip this and join later from the dashboard

**Instructors:**
- You need an **Instructor Access Code** from your department administrator
- Current code: **`MRED-2026`** — keep this private

### Logging In

1. Select your role (Student or Instructor)
2. Enter your email and password
3. You'll be taken to your dashboard automatically

### Password Requirements

- Minimum 8 characters
- At least one letter and one number

## Instructor Guide

### Dashboard

After logging in you'll land on the **Instructor Dashboard** which shows:
- Your active exercises (with Exercise IDs and copy buttons)
- Quick stats (enrolled students, submissions)
- Quick access to Roster, Results, and Live Session

### Creating an Exercise

1. Go to **Exercises** in the sidebar (or click **New Exercise** on the dashboard)
2. Click **New Exercise**
3. Choose a starting template — use **Real Estate Development** to get a pre-built set of activities
4. Customise activities and phase mappings:
   - **Activities**: The cards students will drag and place (title + description)
   - **Phases**: The columns students sort activities into
   - **Weights**: How many points each activity earns when placed in each phase (0–100)
5. Click **Apply** (or **Publish**) to create a live exercise with a unique Exercise ID

> **Tip:** Activities can have weight in more than one phase — a card placed correctly in multiple phases still earns credit.

### Managing Your Roster

Go to **Roster** in the sidebar.

**Option 1 — Join Code:**
1. Click **Generate Join Code**
2. Share the code (e.g. `ASU-2026`) with students
3. Students enter the code when registering — they're auto-enrolled

**Option 2 — Invite by Email:**
1. Click **Invite Student**
2. Enter their email address
3. They'll appear in your roster once they register

### Assigning an Exercise

After publishing an exercise:
1. On the Dashboard, find the exercise card
2. Click **Assign** — a modal shows the Exercise ID and instructions
3. Copy the Exercise ID and share it with students
4. Enrolled students will also see it automatically in their **My Exercises** list

### Viewing Results

Go to **Results** in the sidebar.

- **Exercise Results tab**: Per-student scores for each exercise (Attempt 1, Attempt 2, change, status)
- **Student Histories tab**: Individual student full attempt history
- **Score Trends tab**: Class performance over time

Use **Download Report** (CSV) to export results for gradebook entry.

## Student Guide

### Dashboard

After logging in you'll see your **Student Dashboard** with:
- Exercises available to you (from enrolled instructors)
- Recent scores
- Quick access to History and Scores

### Starting an Exercise

**Option A — From your dashboard:**
1. Click **Start** on any exercise card in **My Exercises**

**Option B — Using an Exercise ID:**
1. Go to **Current Exercise** in the sidebar
2. Enter the Exercise ID your instructor gave you
3. Click **Go**

**Option C — Direct link:**
Your instructor may share a direct link: `https://main.dgai4l6tikxfm.amplifyapp.com/student/exercise?id=<exercise-id>`

### Playing the Exercise

The exercise board has two panels:

**Left panel — Activity Cards:**
- All activities to be placed
- Filter by category: All / People / Task / Test
- Shows remaining unplaced cards

**Right panel — Phase Columns:**
- One column per phase (e.g. Planning, Due Diligence, Construction, Operations)
- Drag a card from the left and drop it into the correct phase column
- A card can go into **multiple phases** if it belongs in more than one

**Controls:**
- **Drag to place**: Pick up a card from the left, drop into a phase column
- **Drag to move**: Pick up a placed card from a column, drop into a different column or drag back to the left panel to remove
- **× button**: Remove a card from a phase column
- **Save Draft**: Saves your current progress without submitting
- **Submit**: Available when all 26 cards are placed (counter shows `26 / 26 placed`)

### Submitting

1. Place all activity cards into phase columns
2. The **Submit** button activates when `XX / XX placed` shows all cards placed
3. Click **Submit**
4. You'll immediately see your score and per-card feedback

### Understanding Your Score

After submitting you'll see:
- **Overall score** (0–100%)
- **Phase Breakdown**: For each phase, which cards were correct (✓), partially correct (◐), or wrong (✗)
  - ✓ **Green** — placed in the primary phase for this activity
  - ◐ **Orange** — placed in a phase with partial credit
  - ✗ **Red** — placed in a phase with no credit
- **Reflection prompt**: A targeted question about your weakest placement

### Resubmitting

After your first submission:
1. Review the feedback — cards are still movable if `locked = false`
2. Adjust placements based on the feedback
3. Click **Resubmit** for your final attempt

> **Important:** You only get **two attempts**. After resubmitting the exercise is locked and no further changes are possible.

### Saving a Draft

Click **Save Draft** at any time to save your current placements without submitting. When you return to the exercise later, your draft will be loaded automatically.

### Viewing History

Go to **My History** in the sidebar to see:
- All exercises you've attempted
- Score for each attempt
- Whether a submission is final
- Click **Details** to see the full per-card breakdown

### Scores Overview

Go to **My Scores** to see a summary chart of all your scores across all exercises.

## FAQ

**Q: I entered the wrong Exercise ID — what do I do?**  
Go back to the exercise selection page and enter the correct ID. If you started the exercise with the wrong ID, you can return to the dashboard and try again.

**Q: I accidentally placed a card in the wrong phase — can I move it?**  
Yes — drag it to the correct column, or click the **×** button to return it to the activity pool on the left.

**Q: Can I leave mid-exercise and come back?**  
Yes — click **Save Draft** before leaving. Your placements will be saved. When you return, they'll load automatically.

**Q: I submitted but want to change my answers before resubmitting — can I?**  
Yes, as long as the exercise isn't locked. After the first submission you can still move cards around before clicking Resubmit. After resubmitting, the exercise locks permanently.

**Q: I don't see any exercises in My Exercises — why?**  
You may not be enrolled in an instructor's class yet. Ask your instructor for a join code and enter it in your profile, or have them invite you by email.

**Q: The Submit button is greyed out — why?**  
All activity cards must be placed in at least one phase before you can submit. Check the counter (e.g., `24 / 26 placed`) — scroll through the activity list to find any remaining unplaced cards.

**Q: My session expired — what do I do?**  
Log out and log back in. Sessions use Cognito JWTs which expire after a set period.
